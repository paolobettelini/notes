\begin{snippetexercise}{primary-module-minimal-generators-direct-sum-exercise}{Minimal generators need not give a direct sum}
    Let \(A\) be a \principalidealdomain and let \(p\in A\) be a
    \primeelement. Suppose a \(p\)-\primarymodule \(M\) is minimally
    generated by two elements \(v,w\), with
    \[
        \moduleann(v)=(p^n),
        \qquad
        \moduleann(w)=(p^m).
    \]
    Must \(M=Av\oplus Aw\) hold? Prove the assertion or give a
    counterexample.
\end{snippetexercise}

\begin{snippetsolution}{primary-module-minimal-generators-direct-sum-solution}{Minimal generators need not give a direct sum}
    The assertion is false. Consider
    \[
        M=
        \left(A/(p^2)\oplus A/(p^2)\right)
        \Big/
        A\bigl(p+(p^2),p+(p^2)\bigr),
    \]
    and let \(v,w\) be the images in \(M\) of
    \((1+(p^2),0)\) and \((0,1+(p^2))\), respectively. They generate
    \(M\), and the defining relation is
    \[
        pv=-pw\neq0_M.
    \]

    Both \(v\) and \(w\) have annihilator \((p^2)\). For example, if
    \(av=0_M\), then in \(A/(p^2)\oplus A/(p^2)\) one has
    \[
        (a,0)=c(p,p)
    \]
    for some \(c\in A\). The second coordinate gives
    \(p^2\divides cp\), hence \(p\divides c\); the first coordinate
    then gives \(p^2\divides a\). The argument for \(w\) is identical.

    The generating family is minimal. Indeed, the defining submodule
    is contained in \(p(A/(p^2)\oplus A/(p^2))\), and hence
    \[
        M/pM\moduleisomorphic (A/(p))^2.
    \]
    The images of \(v,w\) form the standard basis of this
    \(A/(p)\)-vector space. In particular, neither one can be omitted
    from a generating family of \(M\).

    Nevertheless,
    \[
        0_M\neq pv=-pw\in Av\intersection Aw.
    \]
    Therefore \(M=Av+Aw\), but the sum is not direct.
\end{snippetsolution}
